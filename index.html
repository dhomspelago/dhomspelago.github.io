Selected work

An integration that couldn't drop an image

Shockwave Media · INDY TV

Context

A client wanted their in-store screens managed from our platform. Their side exposed the API; ours had to drive it.

What I did

Built the authenticated integration — create, update and delete for images and video — and mirrored every record on our side so a failed push could be retried. When large files broke the first request, I created the record first and attached the media in a background job.

Outcome

Media managed from one place, with failures recoverable instead of silent.

Five seconds to under one

Shockwave Media · INDY

Context

The My Files page had slowed to roughly five seconds as the product data grew.

What I did

Traced it to N+1 queries, then introduced event-driven cache invalidation tied to product updates so the cache could never serve stale data.

Outcome

Load time dropped below one second, and stayed there when products changed.

A legacy system, rebuilt without changing its output

Pictureworks · 2022–2023

Context

What I did
